How the AI captains play

Choose an 80% preference, a 9-turn phase plan, or Score 80%. Phase plans switch after their ninth turn. Each AI uses a legal alternative when its preferred action is unavailable.

18 rounds · 6 / 8 / 10-island maps · provisional starting setup

How to play

After 18 rounds, add one final point for every distinct island with at least one house of your colour. You do not need to control those islands. More own houses on the same island do not add further final points. No houses means zero bonus. Highest total wins; ties share the victory. Each player has 15 building pieces.

Maps for 2–4 players

Two players use 6 islands (I04, I05, I06, I08, I10, I14), three use those six plus I03 and I11, and four add I02 and I09. Every symbol appears twice with two players, 2–3 times with three, or 3–4 times with four. Shuffle the chosen islands into a clockwise circuit. Ships start as evenly spaced as possible: positions 1 / 4, 1 / 3 / 6, or 1 / 3 / 6 / 8.

A: +1 point · B: +2 warehouse seeds · C: +2 water. D: T1/T6 · E: T2/T3 · F: T4/T5. Use a matching public card free, or pay 2 oil for a nonmatching card.

Your turn

  1. A card enters the public market. Above three cards, the oldest is discarded.
  2. Move clockwise: 1 step is free, 2 steps cost 1 oil, 3 steps cost 3 oil.
  3. Land on an opponent’s island: its controller receives 2 warehouse seeds from the supply. Land on your own: receive X water.
  4. Choose one main action: build, rent using a basic effect or one market card, or convert 4 warehouse seeds into 1 fruit. You may also pass.
  5. Optionally plant more seeds, then end your turn.

X equals all houses on this island plus one for each other player's ship currently there. Your own ship does not count. Count ships when each effect resolves; an empty island with another ship has X = 1. Other ships do not change building cost, fixed basic effects, or final island coverage points. Conversion uses four seeds already in your warehouse: move one to your fruit space and return the other three to the supply. It uses your whole main action, so it cannot be combined with building or renting that turn. Planting is a free action between complete effects during your own turn, including before sailing or after landing. You may plant several times. Seeds cannot return to the warehouse. Resolve every point of a single water reward before planting again.

Build with a card

After paying the full 2, 4 or 6 warehouse seeds, place your house and fully resolve its water. You may then discard one public T card for its build bonus: T1 +2 oil; T2 +1 warehouse seed; T3 +1 point; T4 +1 warehouse seed; T5 or T6 +2 water. Any card works on any island. The selected card's travel effect does not apply. A seed gained from the card cannot pay for this same build.

Building and control

Before building, compare your house count on this island with all other players' houses there combined. If yours is greater, pay 2 warehouse seeds; if equal, pay 4; if theirs is greater, pay 6. An empty island costs 4. Ships do not affect the price. The last builder controls the island. Old houses remain. Immediately after building, receive X water, using the new total number of houses plus other ships currently there.

Water and fruit

One water advances one lowest-level seed: field → sprout → fruit. Every field seed must sprout before any sprout ripens. Water cannot be stored, and excess water is lost. Warehouse seeds cannot absorb water.

Travel cards

Use one card from the market, free on its printed symbol or pay 2 oil. Everyone receives X regardless of control: count all houses plus other ships currently on this island. T1 oil, T2 warehouse seeds, and T5/T6 water add X. T3 scores X and grants a fixed 2 warehouse seeds; its seed reward does not increase with X or control. T4 scores 1 + X. You may then use Harvest if ready, affordable, and you have not used a spell this turn. Used cards are discarded without an immediate replacement. All player counts use the same 120-card deck: 20 of each type.

Fruit magic

At most one spell per own turn. Spent fruit immediately returns to your warehouse, and can be spent or planted this turn. Used spells need Rest to refresh.

  • Resupply · 3 fruit: after the spent fruit returns to your warehouse, refill oil and warehouse seeds separately to 4; higher amounts stay unchanged.
  • Pause · 2 fruit, before movement: stay put, skip all landing rewards, then build or rent normally.
  • Harvest · 4 fruit, after your main action: +3 score.
  • Rest · 1 fruit: refresh all four spells, including Rest. It does not restore this turn’s spell allowance.

Final island coverage bonus

Count every distinct island containing at least one house of your colour: each gives you +1 final point, regardless of its controller. Three own houses on one island plus one own house on another give +2 points, not +4. Several players may each earn +1 from the same island when it contains each player's colour. An opponent taking control does not remove your houses or your coverage point. Remaining resources give no additional points.

Captain styles

  • Human player controls their own turn on this device.
  • Build 80% chooses a build preference with 80% probability on each own turn; Rent 80% chooses a rent preference with 80% probability.
  • Build 9 → Rent 9 always prefers building on own turns 1–9, then renting on own turns 10–18.
  • Rent 9 → Build 9 always prefers renting on own turns 1–9, then building on own turns 10–18.
  • Score 80% prefers immediate points on 80% of turns and development on 20%. It considers every legal destination, including islands controlled by the leader.
  • Spread → Score prioritizes its first house on each island during own turns 1–9. It switches to Score 80% after covering every island or completing turn 9, whichever comes first. It uses resource actions when it cannot spread and avoids duplicate building during the spread phase.

The six planned styles share legal movement and resource-management heuristics. They value the marginal coverage bonus: a first own house on an island adds 1 final point; another own house there adds 0. Phase plans do not use an 80% coin. Every planned style falls back to a legal alternative if its preferred action is unavailable, so a build phase does not guarantee a successful build every turn. Each captain slot may use any type, including duplicates.

Provisional setup

Each captain starts with 4 warehouse seeds, 4 oil, no field seeds, sprouts or fruit, 0 points and all four spells ready. Ships start evenly spaced; starting placement has no landing reward. Three cards start in the market. All resources are public. These setup choices come from the provisional rulebook and have not been balance-tested.
